Research

My work centers on school mental health, motivation and behavior change, prevention, and coordinated family–school–community support.

Master’s Thesis · Grit-Enhancement Group Coaching

Designed and implemented a seven-session intervention for upper-elementary students using a three-group quasi-experimental design (experimental, comparison, control; n=24) with pretest, posttest, and two-week follow-up assessments.

Methods: reliability analysis, independent- and paired-samples t tests, mixed ANOVA, repeated-measures analyses, SPSS.

Happiness Habits Scale Development · Undergraduate Research

Initiated the project concept within a four-member undergraduate research team, collected approximately 400–500 survey responses, and contributed to factor-analytic scale development and an internal academic report.

Research Direction

I am particularly interested in how tiered systems of support can connect school mental health, motivation-focused intervention, early prevention, and family–school–community collaboration.
