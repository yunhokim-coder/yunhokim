Research Experience

Kwangwoon University | Master's Thesis Researcher

2022-2024

Designed and implemented a seven-session group coaching intervention, including participant recruitment, pilot testing, program refinement, and two-week follow-up assessment.

Applied a three-group quasi-experimental design (experimental, comparison, control; n=24) and conducted reliability analyses, t tests, mixed ANOVA, and repeated-measures analyses using SPSS; grit showed a significant group-by-time improvement, while self-directed learning effects were not significant.

Kwangwoon University | Undergraduate Researcher

2019

Co-developed the Happiness Habit Scale, beginning with a 26-item pool and analyzing data from 163 young adults; conducted item analysis, internal-consistency analysis, and exploratory factor analysis using SPSS 20.0, resulting in a 15-item, three-factor measure (Agency, Positive Perception of Adversity, and Interpersonal Relationships).

Evaluated the scale’s reliability and criterion-related validity; the initial 26-item measure demonstrated Cronbach’s α = .808 and a significant positive association with the Satisfaction With Life Scale (r = .399, p < .001).
