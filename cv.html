Research Experience

Master's Thesis Researcher, Kwangwoon University

2022-2024

Designed and implemented a seven-session group coaching intervention, including recruitment, pilot testing, program refinement, and two-week follow-up assessment.

Applied a three-group quasi-experimental design (experimental, comparison, control; n=24) and conducted reliability analyses, t tests, mixed ANOVA, and repeated-measures analyses in SPSS; grit showed a significant group-by-time improvement, while self-directed learning effects were not significant.

Undergraduate Research Project - Happiness Habits Scale Development, Kwangwoon University

2019

Initiated the project concept and collaborated on a four-member research team to develop a preliminary psychological measure of happiness-related habits.

Collected approximately 400-500 survey responses, conducted factor-analytic procedures to refine item structure, and co-authored an internal academic paper/report submitted within the university.
