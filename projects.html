Projects

Applied Projects

Selected school- and community-based projects connecting assessment, intervention, mentoring, family support, and program development.

Homecare Psychology Coaching Service Project

Delivered prevention and early-intervention coaching for upper-elementary and middle-school youth from underserved backgrounds, integrating student coaching, caregiver consultation, psychological assessment, and community resources. Conducted internal pre–post program evaluation using SPSS; specific outcome data remain confidential under funder restrictions.

Hwanggok Elementary School Academic Motivation Enhancement Program

Designed the curriculum, activities, and instructional materials and served as lead instructor for an experiential program addressing academic motivation, self-directed learning, learning barriers, and individualized learning strategies.

Green Friends School Cognitive Coaching Group Program

Assistant instructor and project-management assistant for a five-session program serving seven middle-school students with borderline intellectual functioning; supported pre/post assessment using BGT, KFD, HTP, and Egogram measures.

Office of Education–Supported Youth Mentoring Project

Provided brief mentoring and psychological assessment for two adolescents with borderline intellectual functioning, including one out-of-school youth, in collaboration with a social welfare center and Sky-Dream Coaching Psychology Center.

Pet Loss Syndrome Support Project

Managed an annual psychoeducational support project from 2022–2025, integrating group activities, volunteer coordination, interview-based inquiry, and research-informed grief-support materials.
